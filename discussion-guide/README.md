# discussion-guide

Turns research objectives into a moderated interview or usability-test guide.

## What it does

Orders questions from warm-up to core to sensitive, drafts multiple probe directions per question
(not a fixed script), and for usability tests writes task scenarios as goals rather than
click-by-click instructions. States explicitly that it's a guide, not a script — live probing is
the moderator's call.

Consumes `research-plan`'s objectives. Used during fielding, ahead of `synthesis`.

## Usage

`/discussion-guide` with research objectives, or point it at a research plan.
